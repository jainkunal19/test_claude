How to Play

Race up the track and avoid touching any other car — crashing (or finishing outside the top 3) uses up one of your 3 retries for the level; run out and it's game over. Cross the finish line and you score by your placing: 1st = 10,000, 2nd = 7,000, 3rd = 3,000, anything lower = 1,000. Only the top 3 finishers advance to the next level (finish 4th or lower and the run ends). Each level adds more traffic, plus aggressive cars that swerve into you and slower big rigs that block the road.

From level 2 on, grab the ⭐ star on the track (one per level) for a few seconds of invincibility — plow through cars without crashing. A glowing ring on your car counts down the remaining time.

Controls: drag to steer, and drag below the car to brake or above it to speed up (or use ← → and ↑/↓). Your score adds up across levels.
