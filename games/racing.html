How to Play

Race up the track and avoid touching any other car — a single crash ends the game. Cross the finish line and you score by your placing: 1st = 10,000, 2nd = 7,000, 3rd = 3,000, anything lower = 1,000. Each finish advances a level with more traffic, plus aggressive cars that swerve into you and slower big rigs that block the road.

Controls: drag to steer, and drag below the car to brake or above it to speed up (or use ← → and ↑/↓). Your score adds up across levels.
