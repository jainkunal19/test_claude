Snake & Ladders 🎲

Roll the dice and race to 100. Climb ladders 🪜, dodge snakes 🐍. First to land exactly on 100 wins!

Press Start to play
🪜 Ladders lift you up · 🐍 Snakes drop you down · roll a 6 for an extra turn · land exactly on 100 to win

How to Play

Take turns rolling the dice; your token runs forward that many squares from 1 to 100. Land on the foot of a ladder 🪜 to climb up, or on a snake's head 🐍 to slide back down. Roll a 6 and you get another turn. You must land exactly on 100 to win — overshoot and you stay put. In 1-player mode you race the Bot; in 2-player mode it's hot-seat.

Each win is worth 1000 points. Highest Score is your best points total in a run (wins reset when you switch mode, but the best is kept).
