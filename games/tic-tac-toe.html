How to Play

Take turns placing your mark on the 3×3 grid. Get three of your marks in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — to win. In 1-player mode you are X and move first against the bot (Easy, Medium, or Hard); 2-player mode is hot-seat X vs O.

Each win is worth 1000 points. Highest Score is your best points total in a run (wins reset when you Reset Scores or switch mode, but the best is kept).
