How to Play

Drop discs into the columns — they stack from the bottom. Connect four of your color in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to win. In 1-player mode you are red and move first against the bot (Easy, Medium, or Hard); 2-player mode is Red vs Yellow.

Highest Score is the most wins reached in a single run (it resets when you Reset Scores or switch mode, but the best is kept).
