How to Play

Your runner auto-runs down a 3-lane path. As each gate approaches, it shows a math problem with one answer per lane — switch lanes (← → keys, swipe on the board, or the on-screen buttons) to run through the correct one.

Pick right and the lane flashes green, you earn +1 coin, and your correct-answer count goes up. Pick wrong and both your lane (red) and the correct lane (green) flash so you still see the right answer — but it costs −½ heart. You start with 10 hearts; it's game over at 0.

Tap ⚙️ Settings to turn problem types (Addition/Subtraction/Multiplication/Division) on or off, and set an independent Easy/Medium/Hard difficulty for each one.
