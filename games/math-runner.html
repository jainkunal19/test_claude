How to Play

Your runner heads down a 3-lane path. Each gate shows a math problem with one answer per lane — and a monster guarding each one. The runner stops at every question so you have time to work it out.

Tap the answer you want and the runner locks it in and charges straight at it. Prefer to line up first? Swipe across the board or use the ← → keys to shift lanes without committing — when the timer runs out, the runner charges whichever lane it's standing in. Change the thinking time (or switch the wait off entirely for a fast arcade run) under ⚙️ Settings.

Pick right and you slay that creature on the way through, the lane flashes green, and you earn +1 coin. Pick wrong and the monster in your lane lunges and bites you for −½ heart — the runner vanishes and everything holds still for 5 seconds while the correct answer (green, ringed) and a “Correct answer” banner stay on screen so you can learn it. Then he pops back in and runs on. Change that pause (3s / 5s / 8s / Off) under ⚙️ Settings → Answer review. You start with 10 hearts; it's game over at 0.

Watch out as you level up: friendly slimes and ghosts give way to ogres and finally fire-breathing dragons.

Tap ⚙️ Settings to turn problem types (Addition/Subtraction/Multiplication/Division) on or off, set an independent Easy/Medium/Hard difficulty for each one, and choose how long the runner waits at each question.
