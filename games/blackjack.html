How to Play

Beat the House (the dealer bot) by getting closer to 21 than it — without going over. Number cards are worth their face value, face cards (J/Q/K) are 10, and an Ace is 1 or 11, whichever helps.

You get two cards; the House shows one and hides the other. Hit to draw another card or Stand to hold. Go over 21 and you bust (instant loss). When you stand, the House reveals its card and draws until it reaches 17. Closest to 21 wins; equal totals push (tie). A two-card 21 is a Blackjack and pays extra.

Guided mode shows you basic-strategy advice on every turn so you can learn how the pros play — it pays 10 per win. Unguided mode gives no help and pays 100 per win. Highest Score is your best running total in a session.
